## What we ask

We would like to record your dog wearing a small prototype collar (about 15 g) with a
motion sensor, while we film the dog with a phone. The recordings help train a model that
recognizes behaviors such as walking, resting, scratching and shaking.

## What is recorded

- Motion sensor data from the collar (acceleration and rotation).
- Video of the dog during the session, used only to label what the dog was doing.
- Your dog's code name, approximate size, weight and breed. **Not** your name or contact
  details.

## How it is used

- The sensor data and labels may be published in an open dataset for research, under the
  dog's code name only.
- The **video is not published**. It is kept privately for labeling and quality checks.
- The project is open source today and may become a product in the future.

## Your dog's welfare

- Sessions are supervised and short; the collar is removed if your dog seems bothered.
- No behavior is forced or provoked.
- The prototype is not waterproof and is never left on your dog unattended.

## Your rights

- Participation is voluntary. You can stop any session at any time.
- You can ask at any time for your dog's data and videos to be deleted from our records.
  Data already included in a published dataset version will be removed from the next
  version.
